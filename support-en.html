FAQ

Which formats are supported? Image folders, password-free archives (zip/cbz, rar/cbr, lzh/cbl), and page-by-page PDF reading.

How do I read files in a folder consecutively? Open the folder and files continue in filename order. When opening a single file, macOS asks to authorize that folder the first time; afterwards continuation is automatic.

How do I zoom or print? Use trackpad pinch-to-zoom or the zoom menu; press ⌘P to print the current page.

Does the app upload my data? No. It runs locally, makes no network connections and collects no data. See the Privacy Policy.
