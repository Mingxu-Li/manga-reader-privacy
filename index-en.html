Manga Reader Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

1. Overview

Manga Reader (“the App”) runs locally on your Mac and does not collect, upload, or sell any personal data.

2. Data We Collect

The App collects no data – no accounts, no analytics, no advertising tracking, no crash reporting, and no third-party SDKs. The release version makes no network connections.

3. Local Data & File Access

Your library, reading history and settings are stored only in the App’s local data directory on your Mac. The App reads only what you choose to open, within the scope granted by the macOS sandbox. To support continuous reading within a folder, macOS asks you to authorize that folder the first time; the App then reads only inside it, and you can manage or revoke access anytime under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Files and Folders.

4. Third-Party Components

Open-source components and their licenses are listed in the App under Help → Open-Source Licenses.

5. Policy Updates

Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
